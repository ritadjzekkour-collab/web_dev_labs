Hackathon 2026: 48 Hours to Build Smarter Cities

Published on October 12, 2026 by the AI Club team

The AI Club invites all students to its annual hackathon. In teams of three to five, you will design a prototype that makes our cities cleaner, safer, or easier to live in. No prior experience is required: mentors will help you all weekend.
